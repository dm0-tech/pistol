## Statement

*Established.*
- BGW's only admissible partners for an exceptional factor are direct
  sums of one-dimensional Jordan algebras. That is ℝⁿ with the pointwise
  product: the functions on n points, whose states are probability
  vectors. Finite classical probability, i.e. Cox–Jaynes.
- The composite with 𝔥₃(𝕆) is ⊕ₙ 𝔥₃(𝕆): a classical random label choosing
  which octonionic state holds. It supports classical mixing and
  conditioning, and no entanglement.
- **Caveat:** every system has these trivial classical composites.
  The substance is that 𝕆 has no others.

*Reading (conjectural).*
- If any part of reality is octonionic, no quantum probe can become
  correlated with it. Only classical inference can. So classical
  probability is not the rung below the quantum rungs of a ladder
  climbing to 𝕆; it is the only interface to 𝕆.
- The smallest classical partner, ℝ² = functions on S⁰, is the bit that
  survived W-0012's annihilation of nothing and thing.

*Candidates for "the classical system in plain sight" (owner's phrase).*
1. Spacetime points: 𝔥₃(𝕆)-bundles (structure group F₄) over a classical
   base, matching W-0019's base/fibre split.
2. Superselection labels.
3. BGW's own "extra classical bit" in the composite of two complex
   systems.
4. The measurement record (Bohr's classical apparatus).
5. A chosen direction in Im 𝕆. Fixing a unit breaks G₂ to SU(3)
   (machine-checked, dimension 8), so classical data yields an SU(3).

*Flagged hard.* Colour confinement read as non-composability ("only
singlets are seen") could be a physical face of this, or numerology on
SU(3) ⊂ G₂. QCD tensors colour in its state space, so the claim could hold
only at the level of the algebra of observables.
