## Statement

Hegel's *Science of Logic* gives *Aufheben* its twofold sense: to preserve
and to cause to cease; the sublated result loses immediacy without becoming
nothing. Lawvere explicitly proposes adjointly related subcategories as a
mathematical model of unity and identity of opposites, and a way-above
relation between levels as a model of Aufhebung. The source relation is real,
but it is a modeling proposal, not a theorem translating Hegel into category
theory.

The first comparison also has a necessary friction. Hegel says pure being and
pure nothing have their truth in becoming. Lawvere calls the terminal and
initial objects of the trivial level “pure Being” and “non Being,” while the
topos theorem says their isomorphism is degeneracy. Therefore Hegel's
“same” must not be rendered as `∅ ≅ ∗`. The correspondence is useful only
when that non-identity is shown rather than concealed.

## Context

Track H exists to let primary philosophical text generate mathematical
questions without serving as mathematical evidence. Lawvere himself marks the
translation cautiously—“hoping not to do too great an injustice to Hegel”—
and his 1996 abstract says dialectical philosophy can be *modeled*
mathematically. Those verbs set the grade.

## What would change the labels

The source-fact verification is complete for the cited passages. The
correspondence could be strengthened only by a detailed historical and
philosophical argument that survives objections about Hegelian identity,
mediation, and the difference between a logical transition and categorical
minimality. No such argument is claimed here. A reading that equates Hegel's
identity with object isomorphism is refuted by the rung-0 nondegeneracy
theorem and should be disposed, not softened.
