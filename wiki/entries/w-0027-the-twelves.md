## Statement

*Three twelves, established.*
1. **Cyclic, from S³ acting on itself.** π₆(S³) ≅ ℤ/12 is generated by the
   Samelson product ⟨ι, ι⟩, i.e. the commutator map of S³ = SU(2) (unit
   quaternions) with itself (audited, Wockel Prop. III.2). The quaternions'
   non-commutativity, measured against itself, generates the group. Kono:
   SU(2)-gauge groups over S⁴ are classified up to homotopy by instanton
   number mod 12, through this Samelson product (recalled). W-0009's
   e-invariant 1/12 is attached to this generator.
2. **ζ(−1), as a count of possibilities.** χ(SL₂(ℤ)) = 1/4 + 1/6 − 1/2 =
   −1/12 = ζ(−1) (machine-checked: the amalgam ℤ/4 *_{ℤ/2} ℤ/6 against
   −B₂/2). In W-0018's language this is the height-0 cardinality of
   BSL₂(ℤ). It is also the orbifold Euler characteristic of the moduli
   stack of elliptic curves, M₁,₁ = ℍ//SL₂(ℤ) (Harer–Zagier). That is the
   stack whose derived global sections are tmf, the height-2 theory, and
   π₃ tmf = ℤ/24 carries ν (recalled).
3. **Dihedral, G₂'s Weyl group,** of order 12 = 2·6, the product of its
   degrees (W-0026).

*A typed map, established:* Harder's Gauss–Bonnet formula
χ(G(ℤ)) = ∏ᵢ ζ(1 − dᵢ) over the Weyl degrees dᵢ. Machine-checked values:
SL₂ → −1/12, matching the independent amalgam computation; SL₃ → 0;
Sp₄ → −1/1440; G₂ → ζ(−1)ζ(−5) = 1/3024. So Coxeter data (degrees) do map
to ζ-values, and every group's degree 2 contributes the factor ζ(−1). For
G₂, the degrees whose product is the dihedral 12 feed χ(G₂(ℤ)), and one of
the two factors is the ζ(−1) twelve.

*What is not connected, machine-checked:* the dihedral 12 **as a number**.
Among rank-2 types, |W| = 6, 8, 12 (A₂, B₂, G₂), and only G₂ matches
1/|ζ(−1)|. The degree 2 carries the link, and every group has it, so
|W(G₂)| = 12 is currently a coincidence.

*Reading (conjectural, the self-realizing form the owner asked for).*
Twelves 1 and 2 are both SL₂'s: the compact form (S³ commuted with
itself) and the arithmetic form (the modular group counting its own
possibilities to ζ(−1)). They meet on M_ell. That stack counts itself to
−1/12, and its sections (tmf, height 2 in W-0018's ladder) carry the
stable stem where ν lives. A self-referential loop would be: the stack
whose cardinality is ζ(−1) has, as its "functions", a theory whose
homotopy contains the classes whose e-invariants are |ζ(−1)|-valued. That
this loop is a *map*, and not two appearances of B₂, is the conjecture.
