## Statement

Fact 1: in pointed sets, Σ and Ω are trivial (∗ ⊔_X ∗ = ∗ = ∗ ×_X ∗).
Fact 2: a stable ∞-category with discrete mapping spaces is zero
(π₀Map(X,Y) ≅ π₁Map(X,ΣY)). Fact 3: evaluation on the sphere gives
Fun^L(Sp,D) ≃ D for every presentable stable ∞-category D. Thus the free
presentable stable ∞-category on one generator is Sp, with that generator
corresponding to 𝕊 = Σ^∞₊∗. Pointedness, stability, and presentability alone
do not imply D ≃ Sp: derived module categories Mod_HR are other presentable
stable worlds. Selecting Sp therefore additionally selects the free/initial
construction. **A2 is a second axiom.** Reading: Hegel's two moments
of becoming, coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be, which "interpenetrate and
paralyse each other" (§179), correspond to the colimit-built Σ and the
limit-built Ω becoming mutually inverse. Which moment is Σ is a labelling
choice. Pun guard: Miller's "stable unity" (§180) must not be cited.

## Evidence, scoped

- **Claim:** Fact 3, after repair.
- **Ambient setting and hypotheses:** Presentable stable ∞-categories and
  colimit-preserving functors; for the monoidal statement, compatible
  presentable symmetric monoidal structure.
- **Conclusion:** Sp is the free presentable stable ∞-category on one
  generator; evaluation at 𝕊 gives Fun^L(Sp,D) ≃ D.
- **Depends on:** Lurie, *Higher Algebra* Cor. 1.4.4.6 and the cited
  stabilization/initiality results.
- **Evidence, with scope:** Standard universal-property theorem. It
  establishes availability and freeness, not unique selection among all
  presentable stable categories.
- **Additional bridge or choice:** Choose the free/initial construction.
- **What this does not establish:** Pointedness + stability + presentability
  ⇒ D ≃ Sp, or that the ontology selects the free construction.
- **Best current counterexample attempt:** Mod_HR (equivalently the derived
  ∞-category of R-modules) is presentable and stable and is generally not Sp.

## What would change the labels

Upgrade: an argument from the owner's ontology to A2 and to selection of the
free construction (OP-19). Attack: show the Hegel matching is equally good
for a non-stable reading (for example unstable 𝒮_∗ with the smash product),
or that a non-free stable world such as Mod_HR fits equally well.
