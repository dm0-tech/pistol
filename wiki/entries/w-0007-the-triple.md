## Statement

**The proposal (human, verbatim in T-2026-08-20-A):** the summit's
self-reference is likely to *mirror the structural setup of the ladder
ascent* — "the self-reference becomes the becoming (between the two
referents of what has become and what will)" — an intrinsically **ternary**
form, not the binary doubling of W-0003 alone; and the trialities of the
exceptional setting are hoped to cover it, analogously to W-0006's linear
handling of self-transformation.

**Three candidate typings (llm-derived, ordered by how much is already
owned):**

1. **The rung-0 shadow (machine-computed).** The project's first computed
   object already has the ternary form: Ω_𝒮 = ({now, later, never} → {yes,
   no}) — two referents (membership now; membership at the later stage)
   with a middle value, *later*, that is neither: pure deferral, the
   becoming between what has become and what will. One drop of time forces
   a three-valued bit (spec Prop 0.10; non-Booleanness Cor 0.11). In the
   guarded reading (W-0002, OP-13) the middle's engine is ▷.
2. **The ladder's own engine (spec-established form, splice untyped).**
   Every rung runs on an adjoint *triple* i₋ ⊣ i* ⊣ i₊, and the unity of
   opposites is the composite □X → X → ◯X (spec Cor 1.2): the object X
   itself is the third thing, suspended *between* its two modal referents.
   The opposition is binary only if one forgets the thing between. The
   ladder is triples all the way up; the owner's "mirror the ascent" is,
   at minimum, this observation — whether it is *more* than an observation
   is OP-17.
3. **The summit typing (the promotable core).** In d = 3, 4, 6, 10 the
   division-algebra trilinear pairs two spinor representations into the
   vector: at the octonionic rung, 8ₛ ⊗ 8꜀ → 8ᵥ, with the 3-ψ identity
   making the super-bracket {Q, Q} = P consistent (Baez–Huerta) and
   generating the brane-scan cocycles (OP-14, S15). Read against the
   proposal: **two spinorial referents whose bracket is translation — the
   operator that carries what-has-become to what-will-become. The becoming
   is the vector.** Spin(8) triality (S7) is the S₃ making referents and
   becoming exchangeable — the summit as the place where the ladder's
   asymmetric triple becomes a symmetric one. Sub-conjecture (the
   symmetrization ladder): outer-shape triple (F, H distinct, rungs 0–1) →
   ambidexterity (F ≅ H, the linear turn, W-0006's regime) → triality
   (full S₃) — three stages of one symmetrization, mirroring the ascent as
   the owner proposed.

## What would change the labels

- **Cheapest upgrade:** none of typing 3 needs new mathematics to *state*:
  write the trilinear, the 3-ψ identity, and the triality action for the
  d = 3 (ℝ) case in full — small enough to machine-check — and say
  precisely what "the becoming is the vector" asserts there. If it cannot
  be stated at the real rung, it cannot be trusted at the octonionic one.
- **Cheapest attacks:** (a) **E8 has no outer automorphisms** — its
  Dynkin diagram is asymmetric, so no S₃ acts *on E8 itself*; the
  trialities live at Spin(8), at the magic square construction, and at
  𝔥₃(𝕆)'s 3 × 3 structure (S9). "Trialities in the E8 setup" must be
  located there or the phrase is empty. (b) The trilinear exists at *all
  four* division-algebra rungs — if the triple marks every rung, it does
  not mark the summit; the claim must become "mirrors the ascent" (the
  owner's actual phrasing — feature, not bug) and lose any
  summit-exclusivity reading. (c) The identification becoming = translation
  must survive the 𝒮/topos-of-trees variance dictionary (W-0002's open
  step zero); if "later" and step-indexing run in opposite variances, the
  rung-0 shadow may be a pun. (d) Leech at d = 24 is a *triple* 8 with no
  roots (S17's own countermodel): if triples are load-bearing, rootless
  tripling needs an account.
