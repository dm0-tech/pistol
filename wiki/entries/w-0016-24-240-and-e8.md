## Statement

|im J| in stem 4k−1 is the denominator of B₂ₖ/4k (24, 240, 504, 480, 264).
The Eisenstein coefficient −4k/B₂ₖ = 2/ζ(1−2k) equals ±|im J| exactly
while the numerator of B₂ₖ/4k is ±1, and the agreement first fails at k = 6
(691). E8 has 240 roots, and Θ_E8 = E₄. The bridge candidate is geometric:
the E8 plumbing bounds the Milnor sphere generating bP₈ = ℤ/28, whose order
comes from the same B₄.
