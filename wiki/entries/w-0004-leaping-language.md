## Statement

The slogan splits into: (1) **the language is secreted, not imported** —
Mitchell–Bénabou is derived from Ω and exponentials with zero choices, and
it fingerprints the universe's becoming (𝒮 grows the tense "later";
Prop 0.10) [theorem]; (2) **every assertion is made from inside** —
Kripke–Joyal truth is truth-at-a-stage; observer-participancy as semantics
[theorem]; (3) **whether the book narrates its own binding** — whether
Aufhebung is internally statable — is exactly OP-3 [open], permanently
bounded by the diagonal: no topos internally defines its own truth, so the
leap is real but never total; the escapes are stratified (S6) or guarded
(Löb, W-0002). Readings, so marked: Wheeler's pregeometry failed because it
ran the arrow logic→world; the topos-theoretic arrow runs world→logic, and
"it from bit, transformationally" is that correction. The closed loop
Wheeler wanted cannot be a definability fixed point; it may be the ladder —
each rung's language becoming able to *say* what the rung below could only
*do*.
