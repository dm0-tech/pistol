## Statement

(i) 𝕊 → Hℝ keeps π₀ and kills the higher stems, which are all torsion
(Serre). Of the unit, a real-valued valuation keeps counting and nothing
else. Reading Cox this way is conjectural. (ii) Hopf invariant one occurs
only in stems 0, 1, 3, 7, with classes 2, η, ν, σ coming from ℝ, ℂ, ℍ, 𝕆.
The proof is height 1 (Adams operations), and the 1-2-4-8 dimension theorem
follows. (iii) Candidate for OP-11: the codomain terminus (σ, 𝕆) and the
Hypothesis-H side (ν, from the quaternionic Hopf fibration S⁷ → S⁴) are two
stems of one object, 𝕊.

## Context

Thread P's codomain ladder (roadmap §1.3) had its terms as separate codomains; this places them in one object.
