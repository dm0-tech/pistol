## Statement

**The wager (human):** if unit octonions represent possibility itself, and
dimension 16 has room for two full copies of the associated packing, then
possibility can take itself as argument — represent, manifest, bootstrap
itself into existence.

**The stones (all established, audited):** the 240 unit integral octonions
are the E8 roots; E8 is the unique even unimodular lattice in d = 8 and the
densest packing there; d = 16 has exactly two even unimodular lattices,
E8 ⊕ E8 and D16⁺, isospectral but not isometric, and heterotic consistency
forces exactly this pair; independently, gravity amplitudes are the
"square" of gauge amplitudes (KLT/BCJ); on the logical side, Lawvere's
diagonal makes "the universe holding a copy of its own self-transformations
beside itself" (A → Yᴬ, point-surjective) the precondition of every
fixed-point theorem on the bootstrap-stones list.

**The splice (untyped):** whether "containing two independent copies of
one's own possibility-structure" admits a formal statement with the d = 16
doubling as an instance. The exponentials-vs-orthogonal-sums gap is the
honest obstruction.
