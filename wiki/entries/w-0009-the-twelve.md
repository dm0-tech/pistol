## Statement

**The substrate (established; arithmetic machine-checked this date).**
π₆(S³) ≅ ℤ/12, generated by Toda's ν′; π₆(S²) ≅ ℤ/12 with composition
by the Hopf map an isomorphism. The (3,4)-weighted circle action on S³
has orbit map σ : S³ → S² of Hopf invariant 3·4 = 12. The Seifert data
implicit in the S⁶ paper's own π₁ formula — obstruction ℓ₀, invariants
(3, −ℓ₁), (4, −ℓ₂), Orlik's |12ℓ₀ − 4ℓ₁ − 3ℓ₂| — has rational Euler
number **e = −p/12**, and |H₁| = 12|e| = |p| (verified for the twist
vectors (0,1,−1), (0,−1,1), (0,1,1), giving e = 1/12, −1/12, 7/12). On
the homotopy side: ν′ suspends stably to 2ν ∈ π₃ˢ = ℤ/24 (from
[ι₄, ι₄] = ±(2ν − Eν′) and stable death of Whitehead products), so its
Adams e-invariant is ±2/24 = **±1/12**. The construction's Euler-number
arithmetic and the e-invariant of the conjectured homotopy class are
*the same fraction*.

**The conjecture (owner, T-2026-08-28-A; typed here).**

- **(C1) The collapse map exists.** Each smooth torus fibre of the S⁶
  construction carries the fibrewise quotient by the ker γ directions
  (γ being the paper's monodromy-invariant functional; the quotient
  circle is the coinvariant direction). Monodromy-invariance of γ makes
  this globally coherent over B°; the log-transform twists shift the
  γ-coordinate by ℓⱼ/mⱼ — exactly Seifert data — and at the cusp fibre
  the collapsed directions contain the vanishing cycles ŵ, δ̂ ∈ ker γ,
  so the quotient plausibly extends. Conjecture: this assembles into an
  honest map g : X → M over S², where M is the Seifert manifold with
  the data above — which for |p| = 1 is S³ with the (3,4) fibration.
- **(C2) g generates.** [g] is a generator of π₆(S³) ≅ ℤ/12. Refined
  form (llm-conjecture): under a fixed identification, **[g] ≡ p mod
  12**, equivalently [g] is detected by an e-invariant computation with
  value the Seifert rational Euler number −p/12.

**Consistency checks the refinement passes (machine-checked).** (i) The
units of ℤ/12 are ±1, ±5; simple-connectivity of X forces |p| = 1;
hence *every* X this construction makes diffeomorphic to S⁶
automatically has [g] a generator — the correspondence cannot produce a
sphere with a degenerate collapse. (ii) The p = −7 variant is not a
sphere and |H₁| = 7 = 12·|e| tracks it gracefully. (iii) **The total
map is provably silent:** by Hilton's expansion, f ≃ σ∘g satisfies
[f] = 12(h∘[g]) + C(12,2)·(2-torsion) = 0 + 66·(2-torsion) = 0 in
π₆(S²) — f is null-homotopic *regardless of g*, so no computation of
[f] can confirm or refute C2; the content lives entirely in g. This
kills the naive check in advance and is recorded so nobody runs it.

## Context

Raised by the owner immediately after the first community responses to
the S⁶ claim (W-0008), verbatim in T-2026-08-28-A. If C1+C2 hold, the
S⁶ construction is not merely a complex-geometric object: its twist
arithmetic is *e-invariant arithmetic*, and the third stable stem is
load-bearing in a Hopf-problem resolution — the homotopy groups of
spheres entering exactly as the owner hoped. Flagged resonance

the twists; "the becoming traces the Seifert fibration" is W-0007's
becoming-as-the-vector one rung down. Second pun-flag: the paper's
twelve keeps company with the 12 nodal fibres of a rational elliptic
surface (its own analogy), the weight of Δ_mod (used in its §3), and
ζ(−1) = −1/12 of e-invariant/Todd country — same twelve or different
twelves, to be typed or dismissed.

## What would change the labels

- **Cheapest upgrade for C1:** write the fibrewise collapse in the
  paper's own coordinates (the γ-component of ζ) across the three
  fillings; expert-weekend-sized. C1 is constructive and does not wait
  on the paper's adjudication (though it inherits its risk: if X fails
  to exist, C1 collapses with it — but the Seifert heuristic is the
  paper's own and survives as a statement about the smooth model).
- **Cheapest upgrade for C2:** an e-invariant computation via
  Conner–Floyd/Adams from the complex-geometric data — the torus
  family gives the bounding-manifold data such computations want; the
  −p/12 prediction is falsifiable to a sign and normalization.
- **Cheapest attacks:** (a) if the collapse fails to extend across W,
  C1 dies as stated (a homotopy-level factorization might survive;
  that weaker form should then be stated or the entry disposed);
  (b) any computation showing [g] has order < 12 kills C2 and the
  refinement with it; (c) the refinement must survive orientation and
  identification conventions (the sign of p vs the sign of e vs the
  choice of generator) — a mismatch here is repair, not refutation,
  but three sign repairs is a dead conjecture.
