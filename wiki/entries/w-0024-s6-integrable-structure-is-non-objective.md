## Statement

G₂ acts on S⁶ with isotropy SU(3), whose commutant on the tangent space is
span{I, J_𝕆}. Hence ±J_𝕆 are the only invariant almost complex structures,
and they are non-integrable. An integrable structure on S⁶, if one exists
(the Alpöge claim under watch in W-0008), is therefore not G₂-invariant.
