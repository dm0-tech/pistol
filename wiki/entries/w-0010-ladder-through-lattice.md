## Statement

The named modal rungs are not, merely by being drawn in sequence, the
successive Aufhebungen in every ambient topos. Levels form a partial order.
In 𝒮 the complete order is a diamond: the named closed level
`♭ ⊣ ♯` is incomparable with the open level, and the open level—not rung
1—is the Aufhebung of the trivial level. In sSet the levels form the skeletal
chain and rung 1 is the first Aufhebung. Thus the “ladder” is the programme's
chosen path through level lattices; each claimed successive relation requires
an existence, resolution, and minimality proof in the model at hand.

The finite/model-specific statement is established. That all later named
rungs form the intended path in the physically relevant models remains the
programme's conjectural research hypothesis.

## Context

The first roadmap picture presented a linear ascent. The Phase 0 spec did what
a spec should do: its smallest nontrivial model refused the picture and
exposed a diamond. Keeping both words—ladder for the proposed generative
sequence, lattice for the ambient order—retains the programme without turning
its diagram into a theorem.

## What would change the labels

Each later edge upgrades independently when its two levels exist in a named
model and the higher is proved to be the least resolving level. A countermodel
where a named level fails to exist, fails to resolve, or is not minimal does
not refute the computed 𝒮/sSet claim; it revises the programme-wide path.
