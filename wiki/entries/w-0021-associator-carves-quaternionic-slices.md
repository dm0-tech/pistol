## Statement

For imaginary octonions, φ(x,y,z)² + ¼|[x,y,z]|² = |x∧y∧z|², so φ
calibrates exactly the planes on which the associator vanishes. These are
exactly the planes Im ℍ′ for quaternion subalgebras ℍ′ ⊂ 𝕆. In M-theory on
G₂-manifolds, supersymmetric 3-cycles are associative, and M2-instantons
wrap them. Caveat: the brane-scan 𝕆 (spinors in 11d) and the G₂ internal
Im 𝕆 are different appearances, and the link holds only in G₂
compactifications.
