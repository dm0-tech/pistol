## Statement

The exceptional Jordan algebra 𝔥₃(𝕆) is a coherent single-system
octonionic state space. BGW: no composite of Euclidean Jordan algebras has
it as a summand, and no composite exists when one factor has an
exceptional summand, unless the other is classical. So octonionic
possibility is possible alone and impossible in company. Reading: the ℍ
ceiling is a composition ceiling. The selecting requirement is that
independent possibilities combine (Cox's product rule, moved from values to
systems), not associativity of an algebra.
