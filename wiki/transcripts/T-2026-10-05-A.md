## Findings

1. **Classical composites exist and are forced.** BGW Definition 4.1 and
   Propositions 4.3, 4.9 and 4.14 were read in the full paper. For every
   EJA A and finite classical C=ℝⁿ, the componentwise construction
   A C=A^⊕n, π(a,(bᵢ))=(bᵢa), satisfies Definition 4.1. Conversely,
   Proposition 4.9 splits any A(ℝⁿ) into n copies of Aℝ, each canonically A
   by Proposition 4.3. Exceptional systems therefore admit classical
   control but no non-classical partner.
2. **Positivity selects compact octonions twice.** A definite stable
   3-form selects compact G₂ over split G₂. Independently, formal reality
   selects the division-octonion Albert algebra over the split one: a
   nonzero split-octonion null vector gives a nonzero Hermitian X with
   X²=0. No typed map from positivity of plausibility to either condition
   is known.
3. **The obstructions are not currently identical.** BGW's proof is a
   rank/specialness argument. Harvey–Lawson's is the pointwise trilinear
   octonion associator. A common origin in nonassociativity is plausible,
   but requires a map to become a claim.
4. **The S⁴ reading currently adds no constraint.** Mosseri–Dandoloff's
   concurrence coordinates require a two-qubit bipartition, complex
   structure and oriented grouping not intrinsic to bare S⁴. Hypothesis H
   supplies a twisted S⁴-valued cohomotopy cocycle and its flux character,
   but no audited construction selects those extra data. The reading earns
   content only if the twist/equivariance supplies a canonical reduction
   making a concurrence-like pullback gauge invariant and physically
   identifiable.
