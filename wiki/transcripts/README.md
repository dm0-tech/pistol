## Conventions

- IDs: `T-YYYY-MM-DD-X` (X = a, b, … per day).
- Archive files: `T-YYYY-MM-DD-X.md` beside this README.
- "Pending export" is a legal state, but an entry citing a pending
  transcript cannot pass `source-audited` verification until the transcript
  lands — the pointer must be checkable, not just present.
