## What happened

1. The owner asked where the Sati–Schreiber program should go after
   deriving K-theory from M-theory, and asked for "a guess at the only
   geometrically consistent theory of possibility, taking the analogy that
   probabilities are possibilities (à la Jaynes/Cox's theorem)." Without
   having seen this repo, the foil guessed: **stable cohomotopy / the sphere
   spectrum 𝕊**, unique by initiality, with classical probability as the
   height-0 (rational) shadow, K-theory (Snaith: adjoin U(1), invert Bott)
   as "possibilities plus phases" at height 1, and an unnamed "2-dimensional
   amplitude" at height 2. Self-flagged weaknesses were Cox's order and
   density assumptions, "initiality is cheap (why 𝕊 and not ℤ?)", and no
   reduction yet to Vickers' valuation locale at height 0.
2. The owner reported that he and the originating agent had independently
   reached the sphere-spectrum stage, and asked the foil to act as "the foil
   and idea generator … Starting with Lawvere/Hegel, being and becoming."
   The foil's first moves: ∅ ⊣ ∗ as the first opposition; plausibility on
   [0,1] as becoming; the sphere as iterated suspension of nothing; and a
   flagged-speculative "I/∂I ≅ S¹: glue certainty to impossibility to get
   the phase circle."
3. The owner, verbatim: *"Your speculative idea is a pun. There is no such
   thing as nothing. Nor is there any such thing as a thing! These are both
   accidental human constructs."* The circle construction was disposed
   (W-0020). The foil answered that in a stable ∞-category initial and
   terminal coincide (a zero object), so the ontology is the stable
   setting's own axiom, and proposed that the adjunction is the content and
   the endpoints are scaffolding.
4. The owner, verbatim: *"Your suggestion about the start of our ladder is
   incredibly interesting! My adjoint concepts, neither of which are
   actually real :):) -- would be somewhat amazing if this actually told us
   'something'. Please just go for it."* He gave full latitude, including
   over the repo's own rules ("they were just my suggestions").
5. The foil worked the question and filed `notes/no-nothing-no-thing.md`,
   W-0012–W-0020, OP-18–OP-20, and the "no-nothing-no-thing" machine checks
