# Rung 1 → 2 source dossier: cohesion, inner shape, and fracture

*Source audit for the initial rung-2 build, 2026-10-05. This is a research
note, not canon. The proposed formal statements live in
`spec/02-level-2.md`; notation remains governed by `spec/NOTATION.md`.*

## 1. The exact input

Lawvere's *Axiomatic Cohesion*, Definition 2, starts with cartesian-closed
extensive categories ℰ and 𝒮 and an adjoint string

    Π₀ ⊣ Disc ⊣ Γ ⊣ coDisc

(his notation is `p! ⊣ p* ⊣ p* ⊣ p!`). In addition, Π₀ preserves finite
products, coDisc is fully faithful, and a parameterized-powers condition is
imposed. In the topos setting `Disc` is also fully faithful under the usual
connectedness hypothesis. The initial rung-2 slice uses only

1. `Π₀ ⊣ Disc ⊣ Γ`;
2. `Disc` fully faithful; and
3. finite-product preservation by `Π₀`.

It does **not** claim the full Definition-2 package from those three
hypotheses. Source: F. W. Lawvere, “Axiomatic Cohesion,” *Theory and
Applications of Categories* 19 (2007), Definition 2, pp. 43–44,
<https://tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/19/3/19-03.pdf>.

The composites on ℰ are

    ʃ := Disc Π₀,          ♭ := Disc Γ.

Adjunction pasting gives `ʃ ⊣ ♭`. Full faithfulness gives
`Π₀ Disc ≅ id ≅ Γ Disc`, hence both composites are idempotent and have the
same fixed objects, namely the essential image of `Disc`. The constituent
counit of `Disc ⊣ Γ` and unit of `Π₀ ⊣ Disc` supply

    ♭X → X → ʃX.

This is the **inner shape** situation: an idempotent monad and idempotent
comonad with a common fixed subcategory. It is not automatically a
`level` in D0.2.

Shulman's cohesive HoTT gives the corresponding internal reading: shape is
the composite `p* p!`, reflecting into discrete types, while flat is
`p* p_*`; the existence of shape alone does not identify it with
continuous-path shape without an additional real-cohesion/localization
axiom. Source: Michael Shulman, “Brouwer's fixed-point theorem in
real-cohesive homotopy type theory,” *MSCS* 28 (2018), introduction and
§9, arXiv:1509.07584.

## 2. Why this rung is not silently an Aufhebung

D0.2 requires one essential-subtopos triple

    i₋ ⊣ i* ⊣ i₊

and forms its opposed endofunctors from `i₋i*` and `i₊i*`. By contrast,
`ʃ = Disc Π₀` and `♭ = Disc Γ` paste *across two adjacent adjunctions* and
share the inclusion `Disc` as their fixed image. Their variance is also
the inner-shape variance: `ʃ` is the idempotent monad and `♭` the
idempotent comonad.

Therefore D0.3's level order and D0.4's resolution/Aufhebung relation do
not apply merely because `ʃ ⊣ ♭` exists. Calling this “level 2” would erase
a type distinction in the frozen definitions. The project may call it
**rung 2**, but the present result is:

> rung 2 straddles the `Π₀ ⊣ Disc` and `Disc ⊣ Γ` adjunctions; it is not
> established as a level and hence is not established as an Aufhebung.

The Sierpiński model makes the mismatch concrete. For an arrow
`ξ : X₀ → X₁`,

    Π₀X = X₁,    ΓX = X₀,
    ʃX = (X₁ = X₁),    ♭X = (X₀ = X₀).

Thus `ʃ` is the open level's monad `◯_open`, while `♭` is the closed
level's comonad `□_closed`. Those two levels are incomparable
(`spec/01-level-1.md`, Proposition 1.7). The inner opposition is assembled
from one moment of each; it is not the opposition of either level.

## 3. Product preservation: theorem versus bounded evidence

Lawvere includes preservation of finite products by Π₀ as an axiom of
cohesion. For the two repository models it is also directly available:

- **Sierpiński:** `Π₀(X₀ → X₁) = X₁`, so Π₀ preserves products because
  products of arrows are pointwise.
- **simplicial sets:** `π₀(X × Y) ≅ π₀X × π₀Y`. One elementary proof
  regards π₀ as vertices modulo the equivalence relation generated by
  edges. Traverse the X-zigzag while holding Y fixed with degenerate
  edges, then traverse the Y-zigzag while holding X fixed. This avoids
  incompatible edge orientations and proves the nontrivial direction.

The executable sSet check remains deliberately bounded: finite simplicial
sets truncated in dimensions ≤ 2. It checks the formula on named examples;
it is not the proof of the unbounded theorem.

## 4. “Fracture” has hypotheses not present here

There are at least two nearby results that should not be conflated with the
bare unity `♭X → X → ʃX`.

1. **General modal fracture/gluing.** Rijke–Shulman–Spitters, *Modalities
   in homotopy type theory*, §3.4, Theorem 3.50, assumes a lex modality
   `♦` and another modality strongly disjoint from it. Under those
   hypotheses a canonical fracture square characterizes the join of the
   modalities as a pullback. “Strongly disjoint” is an asymmetric
   connected/modal condition, not a consequence of an adjunction
   `ʃ ⊣ ♭`. Source: arXiv:1706.07526v6,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.07526>.

2. **Cohesive modal fracture of higher groups.** Myers' theorem is for a
   **crisp ∞-group** in cohesive HoTT and constructs a fracture hexagon
   whose indicated sequences are fiber sequences and whose squares are
   pullbacks. Crispness, group structure, homotopy fibers, and the HoTT
   ambient theory are substantive hypotheses. Source: David Jaz Myers,
   “Modal Fracture of Higher Groups,” HoTT/UF 2021,
   <https://hott-uf.github.io/2021/HoTTUF_2021_paper_11.pdf>.

Schreiber's differential-cohomology hexagon is stronger in another
direction: its homotopy-Cartesian squares are stated for **spectrum objects**
in cohesive homotopy theory, with stable homotopy fibers and, for the
differential interpretation, further differential cohesion. It supports
the fracture terminology in that stable setting, not for arbitrary objects
of the present 1-topos examples. Source: Urs Schreiber, *Differential
cohomology in a cohesive ∞-topos*, arXiv:1310.7930; see also the theorem
summarized as Theorem 4.4 in “Differential cohomology is cohesive
homotopy,” <https://ncatlab.org/schreiber/files/DiffCoIsCoHo.pdf>.

Accordingly the initial rung-2 theorem says **unity**, not **fracture**.
A later fracture claim must specify which theorem, type the ambient
category/type theory, and discharge its lex/disjointness or crisp-group
hypotheses.

## 5. Pinned obligations

The initial build may establish:

- `ʃ ⊣ ♭`, idempotence, common fixed objects, and unity;
- finite-product preservation of Π₀ in each worked model;
- the Sierpiński formula exactly and finite/truncated sSet evidence;
- the negative typing result: D0.4 does not yet apply.

It must not claim:

- that these hypotheses constitute all of Lawvere cohesion;
- that rung 2 is a level, resolves level 1, or is an Aufhebung;
- a fracture pullback from the bare adjoint triple;
- unbounded sSet verification from the finite script.
